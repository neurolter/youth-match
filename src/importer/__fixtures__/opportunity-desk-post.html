Arab American Leadership Program (AALP) 2027 ($3,000 stipend)

Deadline: December 13, 2026

Applications are open for the Arab American Leadership Program (AALP) 2027. Established in 2021, the Arab American Leadership Program (AALP) is building a career pipeline for emerging leaders to serve in public policy, government and civic leadership. As a fellow of the AALP, you’ll gain hands-on experience through summer fellowships in state or federal offices, or at key institutions across Washington, D.C.

Benefits

  • Networking and mentorship opportunities.
  • Leadership professional development workshops.
  • Firsthand exposure to federal government operations, the legislative process and public policy development.
  • Fellows will be provided a stipend of $3,000. Assistance for transportation and housing is also available to those who qualify based on financial need (up to $2,000).

Eligibility

  • Be at least 20 years old (or will be by May 1);
  • Be currently enrolled at an accredited college/university in the United States OR have previously graduated from such program;
  • Have a cumulative GPA of 3.4 or higher.

Application

Deadline to apply is December 13, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET.
