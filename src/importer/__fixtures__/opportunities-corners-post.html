Leadership and Peace Summit Morocco 2027 (Fully Funded)

Applications are open for the Leadership & Peace Summit Morocco 2027, an international summit bringing together emerging leaders, young professionals, students, changemakers, and peace advocates from around the world. Organized by the Voice for Rights International (VFRI), the summit will focus on leadership, peacebuilding, diplomacy, cross-cultural understanding, and global cooperation. The Leadership & Peace Summit Morocco 2027 will take place from 29 to 31 January 2027 in Morocco.

Quick Overview

  • Event: Leadership & Peace Summit Morocco 2027
  • Dates: 29–31 January 2027
  • Location: Morocco
  • Organizer: VFRI
  • Deadline: 1st December 2026

Summit Theme

The summit is being held under the theme “Empowering Leadership, Advancing Peace, and Strengthening Global Cooperation.”

Application Fees

  • $23
